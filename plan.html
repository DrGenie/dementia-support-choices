    How these numbers are calculated

    All results on this page come from the mixed logit choice model estimated from the choices of 264 Australian adults. The model represents the population with 1,000 simulated people whose preferences vary in the way the study found. Take-up is the average, over these people, of the chance of choosing the programme rather than no programme. The 95% intervals repeat the calculation for 1,000 redrawn sets of estimates.

    The value to families is the money value of having the programme available compared with no programme, net of the fee, averaged over everyone offered it. The break-even running cost is the provider cost per participant per session at which that value, plus the fees paid, equals the cost of running it. Benefits and costs shows the full calculation.

    These are predictions of stated choices, not forecasts of attendance at a particular service. They do not include waiting lists, local availability or how a programme is promoted. Fees outside A$0 to A$50 per session were not tested and cannot be selected. Terms are explained in the glossary.

    About this website

    Dementia Support Choices presents the results of a discrete choice experiment with 264 Australian adults, carried out by researchers at the University of Newcastle. It gives information from research. It is not medical advice and does not replace advice from health professionals, aged care assessors or service providers.
