How a fee changes take-up

Lines show predicted take-up against no programme as the fee changes; shaded areas are 95% intervals. Move the pointer over the chart to read values.

Sensitivity to price

Price elasticity is the percentage change in take-up for a 1% increase in the fee. Values between 0 and -1 mean take-up falls by proportionally less than the fee rises.

Why there are three lines

The multinomial logit with a straight-line cost treats every dollar as equally off-putting. The piecewise model lets the first dollars count for more than later ones, which the data supported: moving from free to A$5 per session reduced appeal about as much as moving from A$20 to A$50. The mixed logit allows people to differ, including in their willingness to use any programme at all.

About this website

Dementia Support Choices presents the results of a discrete choice experiment with 264 Australian adults, carried out by researchers at the University of Newcastle. It gives information from research. It is not medical advice and does not replace advice from health professionals, aged care assessors or service providers.
