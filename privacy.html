Privacy

This website does not collect, send or store your personal information.

What we do not collect

  • There are no accounts, forms that send data, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Answers you give in "What matters to you" and the programme and fee tools are calculated in your own browser. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Fonts and all other files are served from this website, so your browser does not contact third-party services when you visit.

What is stored on your device

If you change the text size, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that the next page opens at the same size. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to mesfin.genie@newcastle.edu.au.

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands on which this research was carried out, and pay our respects to Elders past and present.

About this website

Dementia Support Choices presents the results of a discrete choice experiment with 264 Australian adults, carried out by researchers at the University of Newcastle. It gives information from research. It is not medical advice and does not replace advice from health professionals, aged care assessors or service providers.
